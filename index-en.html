HahaFit

Plan push-ups, pull-ups, crunches, and squats on your iPhone. The camera counts repetitions in real time on your device, while workout plans and records stay on your device.

Download for iOS

Available on iOS

  • Push-ups
  • Pull-ups
  • Crunches
  • Squats

How it works

  • Work out with the camera

    Each completed repetition is counted on your device. Camera frames are not saved or sent to a recognition service.

  • Get reminders when you choose

    Set your training days and time. Reminders run on your device and stop after you complete the workout.

  • Keep workout data on your phone

    There is no account. Plans and completion history are stored locally and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
