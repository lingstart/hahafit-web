HahaFit User Agreement

Effective date: September 30, 2026

HahaFit helps you plan and complete push-ups, pull-ups, crunches, and squats on your iPhone. By using the app, you agree to this agreement and the Privacy Policy.

No registration

HahaFit has no account, nickname, or profile. Workout plans, completion records, and reminders are stored on your device and are not synced to other devices.

Workout guidance

The app recognizes movements and counts repetitions locally with your camera. It is a workout aid, not medical, rehabilitation, or professional coaching advice. Train within your ability; if you have an injury or discomfort, consult a clinician and stop exercising.

Permissions

  • The camera is used only during workout recognition. Frames are not saved or uploaded.
  • Alarm and notification permissions are used only for the workout reminders you configure.

You can disable permissions in iOS Settings. Without camera access, repetition counting is unavailable; without alarm or notification access, reminders will not be delivered.

Your data

You may archive or delete workouts in the app. Deletion removes the corresponding local workout plan and completion history and cannot be undone. Uninstalling the app also removes local app data. See the Privacy Policy for details about local storage and analytics.

Contact and changes

You can send feedback to ling_start@126.com. We may update this agreement when app functionality changes and will revise its effective date. Continued use means you accept the updated agreement.
